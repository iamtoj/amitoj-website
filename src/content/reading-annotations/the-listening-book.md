<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">What Stayed</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>Close the eyes. Sustain a note. Walk until rhythm stops feeling added and starts organizing the movement. Mathieu’s exercises make listening an action rather than a channel left open.</p>

<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Argument</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>He moves through hum, breath, resonance, improvisation, and attention, then joins those exercises to a larger account of vibration connecting inner and outer life. The observable change is modest: attention alters what becomes audible. His claim that musicality is innate is an interpretation of that change, not a result the exercise can decide.</p>
</div>
