<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">What Stayed</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>In tree pose, balance sometimes changes before I can name the correction. Breath drops, attention softens, and the body adjusts without waiting for an instruction from the head. Shepherd’s distinction between observing the body and attending through it gives that shift a precise form.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Argument</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>He locates the neglected intelligence in the pelvic bowl and argues that Western culture learned to privilege head-centered analysis. The exercise lets me examine the first claim from practice; it cannot establish the historical story. That asymmetry is the book’s useful pressure: I can test where attention sits without accepting an account of how an entire civilization came to put it there.</p>
</div>
