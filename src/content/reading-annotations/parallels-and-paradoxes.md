<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">Why I Recommend This</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>A Palestinian literary critic and an Israeli conductor meet in a London hotel lobby. What could've been one awkward conversation became a decade-long friendship. They founded an orchestra of young Arab and Israeli musicians. They argued about music, performance, Wagner's anti-Semitism, what art can do that politics can't.</p>
<p>This book is transcripts of those conversations. What stayed with me: their insistence that understanding does not require agreement. You can hold your position while genuinely engaging with someone who opposes it. Barenboim says the orchestra is against ignorance specifically, which sets a different bar than "for peace." The distinction matters.</p>
</div>
</section>

<!-- THE BOOK -->
<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Book</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>Emerged from public conversations at Carnegie Hall and private dialogues over a decade. Said (the author of <em>Orientalism</em>) and Barenboim (conductor, pianist, citizen of Argentina, Israel, and Spain, with honorary Palestinian citizenship) talk about music, performance, Wagner's anti-Semitism, and what art can do that politics cannot.</p>
<p>In 1999, they founded the West-Eastern Divan Orchestra, bringing together young Israeli and Arab musicians.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">Passages That Stayed With Me</h2>
<div class="space-y-6">
<blockquote class="border-l-2 border-sage-400 pl-6">
<p class="text-lg italic mb-2">"You have to find a way to put the extremes together, not necessarily by diminishing the extremity of each one, but to form the art of transition."</p>
<p class="text-sm text-ink-muted">Barenboim on music. I keep testing the “art of transition” elsewhere; the book does not show that it applies everywhere.</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote class="border-l-2 border-sage-400 pl-6">
<p class="text-lg italic mb-2">"The Divan was conceived as a project against ignorance... to understand what the other thinks and feels, without necessarily agreeing with it."</p>
<p class="text-sm text-ink-muted">The target is ignorance, specifically.</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote class="border-l-2 border-sage-400 pl-6">
<p class="text-lg italic mb-2">"No one can exactly interpret a composer's score because the spirit is not on the page but in the making."</p>
<p class="text-sm text-ink-muted">The notation is not the music.</p>
</blockquote>
<blockquote class="border-l-2 border-sage-400 pl-6">
<p class="text-lg italic mb-2">"Music can be the best school for life, and at the same time the most effective way to escape from it."</p>
<p class="text-sm text-ink-muted">Engagement and transcendence, held together.</p>
</blockquote>
</div>
</section>
