<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">What Stayed</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>Novelty search rewards difference from what has already been found, not progress toward a target. In the evolutionary-computation problems Stanley and Lehman report, that change found solutions objective-driven search missed because useful intermediate states did not resemble the goal.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Argument</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>The experiments establish how objective pressure can hide stepping stones inside those search spaces. Careers, organizations, education, and science funding are not the same search space. The book’s examples invite that extension; the experiments do not perform it.</p>
</div>
