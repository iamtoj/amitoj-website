<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">What Stayed</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>Cameron asks for three handwritten pages each morning. In my first month, recurring concerns became easier to see on the page.</p>
<p>Her useful reframe is creativity as recovery rather than production on command. I use the pages as an instrument for noticing what recurs, not as a guarantee that a hidden creative self will emerge.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Argument</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>Cameron’s twelve-week program rests on Morning Pages and weekly Artist Dates. She treats fear, criticism, and conditioning as blocks to an existing creative capacity.</p>
<p>The practices shift attention from evaluating a finished product to maintaining a creative life. Their spiritual interpretation belongs to Cameron’s framework; the exercises can still be observed through what they change for the person doing them.</p>
</div>
</section>
