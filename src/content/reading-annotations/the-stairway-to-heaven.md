<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">What Stayed</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>The same contemplative instruction can steady one person and unsettle another. Springett’s map matters because it makes fit, not universality, the first question I ask of a practice.</p>

<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Argument</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>She arranges development into nine stages and applies the map across psychotherapy, Buddhist practice, relationships, work, and spiritual life. The cases show how she interprets difference. They do not validate the map as an assessment, and I would not use it to place another person on a universal scale. Its practical use is a question: what conditions must be present for this instruction to help?</p>
</div>
