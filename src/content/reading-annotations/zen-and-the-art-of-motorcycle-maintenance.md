<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">What Stayed</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>Asking whether analytical or intuitive understanding is superior begins after the split Pirsig wants to explain. Quality is his attempt to locate what comes before subject and object, classical and romantic, thought and feeling.</p>
<p>Motorcycle maintenance carries the inquiry because care is visible in the work without belonging exclusively to either side. An organization may invite comparison, but the book supplies no general organizational mechanism. Its foundation is the prior relation between attention, care, and the thing being worked on.</p>
</div>
</section>

<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Argument</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>The book joins a motorcycle journey, philosophical Chautauquas, and the narrator’s confrontation with Phaedrus, his earlier self. Motorcycle maintenance becomes the recurring practice through which analytical precision and direct experience meet.</p>
<p>Pirsig calls their prior ground Quality. He resists a fixed definition because definition would return the inquiry to the subject-object division it is meant to explain. The result is a metaphysics carried by an act of care rather than a concept detached from practice.</p>
</div>
</section>
