<section class="mb-12">
<h2 class="text-sm text-ink-muted uppercase tracking-wider mb-4">The Argument</h2>
<div class="prose prose-lg">
<p>He joins dissipative structures, autopoiesis, hypercycles, and Gaia in an evolutionary account that moves from physical systems through organisms to culture and consciousness. The continuity is his wager. Chemistry shows that order can arise far from equilibrium; it does not by itself establish one causal mechanism for organizations. The question I carry is narrower: what was organizing here before anyone announced the change?</p>
</div>
